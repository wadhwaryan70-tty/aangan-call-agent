Where each call ends up

Four outcomes from the classifier

Qualified

Book and hand off

  • Consultation booked inside the call
  • Handoff email to the matched designer
  • HubSpot deal created
  • Reminder to caller the day before
Not a fit

Close politely

  • Explains what Aangan does, from services.md
  • No slot offered
  • Logged in Supabase for Nikhil's weekly review
Asks for a price

The Cut

  • Agent says: "Your designer will walk you through pricing on the consultation."
  • No number, no range, no per-sq-ft rate
  • Pricing question flagged in the handoff note
Complaint or edge case

Pass to a human

  • Agent takes a message, makes no commitment
  • Email to the front desk for first thing next morning
Boundary · checks 06, 07, 09

What the agent does, and where a human stays

Agent does

  • Answers every call within seconds, day or night
  • Asks the intake questions
  • Qualifies against qualified.md
  • Books pre-set consultation slots
  • Writes the handoff note

Human only

  • Quoting any price (Nikhil owns the price)
  • Committing to site visits or timelines
  • Handling complaints
  • The client relationship (designer owns it)

Owners

  • Agent qualifies and books
  • Designer owns the meeting and relationship
  • Nikhil owns the price and the rules in qualified.md
Handoff choice

Email through Resend, not Telegram

Email · Subject: New consultation Thu 8 Oct, 4:00 pm: Priya S., 3BHK, Baner 🏠 New consultation: Thu 4:00 PM Priya S. · Baner, Pune · 3BHK, ~1,400 sq ft Scope: full home interiors Budget band: ₹10–12L · Possession: Jan Fit: Qualified (residential, full scope, in service area) Asked about pricing → told designer will cover it
Claim this leadRescheduleRecording

Every designer already lives in their inbox, and an email is a record Nikhil can search, forward and audit later.

  • The subject line carries the slot. A designer sees when, who and where in the notification without opening it.
  • One-tap Claim. The first designer to claim takes the lead, so two people never chase the same client. Links are signed so only the email's recipient can use them.
  • Nothing new to install. No bot, no group to manage, no personal phone numbers shared.
  • Speed trade-off, handled. Email is slower than chat, so the client already has a booked slot before the email lands. The designer is preparing, not racing.
