Where each call ends up

Four outcomes from the classifier

Qualified

Book and hand off

  • Consultation booked during the call
  • Email to the matched designer
  • Annoyed prospects still qualify, with a "Handle with care" note
Not a fit

Close politely

  • Other city, advice only, hospitality, too small, under 6 weeks, or a volunteered budget clearly too low
  • No slot offered; logged for Nikhil
Asks for a price

The Cut

  • Agent says: "Your designer will walk you through pricing on the consultation."
  • Never disqualifies; flagged in the note
Complaint or AI failure

Pass to a human

  • Existing client with a problem: message taken, no promises
  • Front desk emailed; if the AI step fails, the raw transcript is emailed so no lead is lost

What the agent does, and where a human stays

Agent does

  • Answers every call within seconds, day or night
  • Asks the intake questions
  • Qualifies against qualified.md
  • Books a consultation slot
  • Writes the handoff note

Human only

  • Quoting any price (the Cut)
  • Committing to site visits, start or finish dates
  • Handling existing-client complaints
  • The client relationship

Owners

  • Agent qualifies and books
  • Designer owns the meeting and relationship
  • Nikhil owns the price and the rubric
Handoff choice

Email through Resend, not Telegram

Email · Subject: New consultation Mon 12 Oct, 12:00 pm: Neha, 3BHK, Baner Wants complete interiors for a new home. 3BHK in Baner (~1,300 sq ft), possession received. Timeline: move-in target by February. Budget not specified. Deciding jointly with husband; authorised to set up. Asked about pricing; told designer will cover it.
Claim this leadRescheduleRecording

Designers already work from their inbox, and an email is a record Nikhil can search, forward and audit.

  • The subject line carries the slot. The notification alone says when, who and where.
  • One-tap Claim. The first designer to claim takes the lead. Links are signed, and a confirm step stops email scanners from clicking them.
  • Nothing new to install. No bot, no group to manage, no personal numbers shared.
  • The speed trade-off is covered. The caller already has a booked slot before the email lands.
