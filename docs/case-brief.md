# Case 03: Aangan Studio, unanswered enquiries

## Why

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pain** | ~48% of ~200 monthly enquiries get no reply within 48 hours. A third arrive outside 10am–7pm. A reply within an hour converts at 4× a next-day reply. Average project ₹8–14 lakh. |
| **User** | Callers (prospective clients); designers (14) who take the first consultation; Nikhil (founder) who owns pricing and wants the numbers without counting by hand. |
| **Outcome** | Every call answered in under 5 minutes, day or night. Qualified callers leave the call with a booked consultation. Designers get everything already asked. Nikhil sees volume, conversion and running cost on one page. |

**Scope:** incoming phone calls only. WhatsApp and the web form reuse everything except the trigger and input (not built).

## The journey today

1. A caller rings the studio number. After 7pm, or when both front-desk staff are busy, nobody picks up.
2. If someone answers, they ask the intake questions, then forward a bare message to a designer.
3. The designer calls back, often the next day, and asks the same questions again.
4. Nikhil counts enquiries by hand at month end.

## The Nine Checks

### ⚡ Kill switches (any No means: don't build it)
- **01 Problem real? YES.** 48% unanswered for 48+ hours; 4× conversion under one hour. Nikhil's own numbers.
- **02 Workflow repeated? YES.** ~200 calls a month; the first four steps are identical every time.
- **03 Input available? YES/PARTLY.** services.md and qualified.md are enough to answer and qualify. pricing.md exists but cannot be used for output. Designers' consultation slots must be set up on a shared calendar.

### 📊 Sizing (any No means: build something smaller)
- **04 Output valuable? YES.** An acknowledgement and a consultation booked inside the call on an ₹8–14 lakh project is directly revenue.
- **05 Impact measurable? YES.** Time to first response, % under one hour, consultations booked, show-up rate. All baselineable on day one.
- **08 ROI worth it? YES.** ~96 calls a month unanswered. A modest lift covers the tool cost many times over.

### 🚧 Boundary (any No means: put a human exactly here)
- **06 Failure risk OK? YES** for answering, qualifying and booking (a wrong slot is fixed with a reschedule). **NO for pricing:** a price said in the studio's voice is heard as a commitment. Unrecoverable.
- **07 Judgment protected? PARTLY.** Answering, qualifying and booking pre-set slots: assistable. Quoting, committing to site visits or timelines, handling complaints: human only.
- **09 Owner clear? YES,** once bounded. The agent qualifies and books. The designer owns the relationship. Nikhil owns the price.

**The Cut:** quoting standard per-square-foot pricing. The agent says "Your designer will walk you through pricing on the consultation."

## Handoff: email, not Telegram

- Designers already live in their inbox; nothing new to install or manage.
- The subject line carries the slot, so the notification alone tells the designer when, who and where.
- One-tap Claim (first designer wins) stops two people chasing one lead.
- Email is a searchable record Nikhil can forward and audit.
- The speed trade-off is covered because the client already has a booked slot before the email lands.

## Results

20 / 20 real September phone enquiries classified correctly on the live app, with no price in any output. Two fixes came from the eval: the classifier now gets the date of the call, and "complaint" means an existing client, so an annoyed new prospect still reaches a designer. See `eval-results.md`.
